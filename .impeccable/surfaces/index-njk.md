## Scope

The one-page recruitment site (`index.njk`, `styles.css`, `script.js`). Visitor mode: Persuade. Phone-first: most visitors open a WhatsApp link.

## Audience, job, action

American Chassidish families (in Eretz Yisroel and in America) judging whether the moshav is real, affordable and right for them; the action is the sign-up form. Proof on hand: live family/children counts, prices, stages, history, statistics with sources. No real photos, testimonials or named founders; images are illustrative renderings and stay labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a new community, done at Serenbe/Kiawah River craft: the place leads, the words are few and calm, and the sign-up is never more than a thumb away. It refuses the AI cream-serif-terracotta look and the developer pop-up/promo look.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with a cool off-white band (#f5f6f4), charcoal ink (#1f2328), one warm brass accent (#a8834a) reserved for the primary button and small marks; photography carries all other colour. Libre Caslon Text at light weight for headlines, Assistant for text, Frank Ruhl Libre for Hebrew/Yiddish. Square corners, hairline rules, no cards.

STORY: The visitor sees the hills and the homes, learns in one line what this is and where, sees that 74 real families have already joined, understands the price advantage and the three stages, and signs up their family.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photograph; slim white nav (name left, menu right). Headline bottom-left in white light serif, one line of subcopy, two buttons side by side: solid white "Become a founding family" and outlined "Download the brochure". "Illustrative concept" note bottom-right. Directly below the photo, a white band with the live counts.

FORM: The category standard (canon), chosen by the user over the roll; seed: 09b3cce63191a419.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
