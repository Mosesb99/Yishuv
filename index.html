Our vision

What we're trying to build

Yishuv means "settlement" — a place people choose to build, rather than simply move into. That's the heart of what we're doing. Instead of buying a house and hoping the neighbours turn out well, we're bringing together like-minded families first, and then building the neighbourhood around them.

We envision a community of roughly [number] households in [location], with homes, shared spaces, and the institutions a community needs to thrive — a place for prayer and gathering, education for our children, and room to grow.

It will be a place where Shabbat and festivals are celebrated together, where a new baby means a week of meals arriving at the door, where elders are honoured, and where every family has a real voice in how the community grows.

Community life

What kind of community it will be

These are the values we're building on. If they resonate with you, you'll probably feel at home here.

Real neighbours

Homes are planned close together around shared green space, so children can play freely and doors stay open.

Rooted in tradition

Jewish life and values are at the centre — Shabbat, holidays, learning and prayer are shared experiences, not solo efforts.

Education & growth

Strong education for children and ongoing learning for adults, with the aim of local schooling as the community grows.

Mutual support

Meal trains, shared childcare, a free-loan fund, and help for whoever needs it. Nobody goes through hard times alone.

Welcoming & warm

Families at every stage — young couples, growing families, retirees — each bringing something to the whole.

Shared decisions

Founding members shape the community: layout, amenities, guidelines and how we govern ourselves going forward.

Is it for you?

Who we're looking for

You don't need to have everything figured out. You might be a good fit if you:

  • Want your family to live inside a close, values-driven community
  • Are ready to give time and energy, not just receive
  • Value Jewish life, learning and tradition
  • Are excited, not daunted, by building something from the ground up
