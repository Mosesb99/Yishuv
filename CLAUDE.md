# Yishuv website — project context

This file carries over the conversation so far, so any new Claude session picks up where we left off.

## What this is

A recruitment website for **Yishuv**, a new community in Israel being built for families making
aliyah. Static site: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, `config.js` (form endpoints),
`assets/favicon.svg`. No build step. See `README.md` for hosting and form setup.

Current site is a **first draft** with generic copy and `[bracketed]` placeholders. The owner wants
a **premium** version rebuilt around the real story below.

## Positioning (from the owner)

**Audience:** frum (Orthodox) families from American communities like Monsey who want to make aliyah.

**The problem:** they're used to single-family houses with gardens and spacious landscape, and
aliyah usually means a cramped apartment among tons of buildings in Israel's big centres.

**What makes a visiting family say "this is it":** the spacious landscape, and seeing that a home
here costs *less* than a cramped apartment in one of the big centres.

**What makes it different from moving to an existing frum neighbourhood:**
- A tight-knit community, which you don't get in most places in Israel
- Price
- Landscape and way of living
- People are non-judgmental and open-minded, there to help the community
- Hard-working people (owner said "workaholics" — use "hard-working" on the site) who still keep a
  healthy work–family balance

**Draft headline (proposed, owner hasn't confirmed yet):**
> Make aliyah without giving up your backyard.
> A tight-knit, open-minded frum community in [region] — spacious homes with gardens, for less than
> a cramped apartment in Israel's big cities.

**Three pillars to build the site around:** Space · Value · Community.

## Still waiting on the owner

1. Location — region / nearest town, distance to Jerusalem, Beit Shemesh, airport; is land secured?
2. Prices — rough home cost and size (bedrooms, garden) vs. what that buys in a big city. Plan a
   price-comparison section; it's likely the most persuasive part of the site.
3. Religious character — Chareidi / Yeshivish / Modern Orthodox / mixed?
4. Audience — mainly Anglo olim or Israelis too? English only or English + Hebrew?
5. Who's behind it — founding families, Rav, developer; names/photos?
6. Parnassah — remote work for US jobs, commute, internet, shared office?
7. Timeline and numbers — families interested, homes planned, first move-in date.
8. Also: logo, photos/renderings of the land, contact email/phone, who receives sign-ups,
   whether an actual brochure PDF exists.
